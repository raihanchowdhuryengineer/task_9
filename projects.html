Projects

The three products I designed and built this year.

Brew & Bloom advert

  • Animation
  • Adobe Animate
  • Audacity

A 15-second internet advertisement for a new café, aimed at students and young workers. It uses motion tweens and an Order now button that links to online ordering.

What I learned: strong contrast makes a call to action easy to find.

Harmony Night poster

  • Event product
  • Illustrator

A digital poster advertising the school music night to Year 10–12 students, planned with thumbnails and wireframes and tested by two classmates.

What I learned: peer feedback showed the date needed to be larger, so I changed it.

Design exercises

  • Digital portfolio
  • Spreadsheets

Exercises on vector and raster graphics, audio files, a VLOOKUP price table, design analysis of existing works and layout grids.

What I learned: use vector for logos and raster for photos.
